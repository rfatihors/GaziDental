# Intra-observer reliability of the clinical reference (calibration.xlsx)

Gingival display was measured twice by the same observer on 20 images, at six tooth sites per image (sheets `İlk Ölçümler` / `İkinci Ölçümler`, same ×1000 coding). The measured quantity is the gingival display at a tooth site, not a dimension of the tooth. 2 of the 20 images are in the current COCO set.

| level | n | ICC(2,1) [95 % CI] | ICC(3,1) [95 % CI] | ICC(2,k) | mean diff (2−1), mm | SD, mm | 95 % LoA, mm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tooth site | 120 | 0.995 [0.994, 0.997] | 0.995 [0.994, 0.997] | 0.998 | 0.012 [-0.019, 0.042] | 0.167 | -0.32 to 0.34 |
| image mean | 20 | 0.998 [0.995, 0.999] | 0.998 [0.995, 0.999] | 0.999 | 0.012 [-0.029, 0.052] | 0.086 | -0.16 to 0.18 |

The tooth-site-level SD of 0.17 mm is the observer's own repeatability floor; the paired t-test is not used as evidence of agreement (ICC and limits of agreement are).
Expected orders of magnitude (audit §B): ICC ≈ 0.995 / 0.998, SD ≈ 0.17 mm.
